Website making is the art of turning ideas into interactive digital spaces. It involves combining structure with creativity—using HTML to build the foundation, CSS to style and shape the look, and JavaScript to add dynamic features. A well‑made website is more than just pages; it’s a platform that connects people, showcases your vision, and provides a smooth experience for visitors. Whether for business, learning, or personal projects, website creation is the gateway to sharing your world online.
